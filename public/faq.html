Frequently asked questions

Fees, duration and how we work. If your question isn’t here, email hello@nxtfoundry.in.

What is your consulting fee?

Every programme is fixed-fee, scoped to your situation and agreed in writing before we start. There is no hourly billing. Get in touch and we’ll share the fee for the programme that fits after a first conversation.

How long does a consulting engagement typically last?

The Diagnostic takes half a day. The Reset runs 30 or 60 days with weekly on-site visits. Ongoing Advisory is monthly, with a three-month minimum, for as long as it stays useful. Custom engagements have an end date agreed upfront.

Which programme should I start with?

Most clients start with the Diagnostic. If you already know the problem and have tried to fix it, the Reset is usually the right place. If you don’t need a project — just a senior sounding board — start with Ongoing Advisory. Not sure? Talk to us and we’ll tell you honestly.

How is payment structured?

Payment terms are fixed and agreed before the work begins — no open-ended billing, no surprise invoice at the end. We’ll walk you through them when we scope the engagement.

Can the engagement be done remotely?

Partly. The Diagnostic and Ongoing Advisory work well online. The Reset is built on weekly on-site visits, because operating problems are fixed where the work happens, not on a call. Outside Bengaluru, we agree the on-site rhythm with you before we start.

Who will actually do the work?

We do, personally. NXT Foundry is senior-led, not staffed out. You won’t be handed to a junior consultant after the first meeting.

What size of business do you work with?

Owner-led and professionally managed businesses, typically in the ₹50 Cr – ₹1,000 Cr revenue range. If you sit outside that, tell us — we’ll say honestly whether we’re the right fit.

Will you sign an NDA?

Yes. We’re happy to sign your NDA before the first detailed conversation, or share ours. What you tell us stays with us.

Do you offer business mentoring?

Yes — that is what Ongoing Advisory is. A senior thinking partner for founders and leadership teams, on call for the decisions that matter, without a project attached.
