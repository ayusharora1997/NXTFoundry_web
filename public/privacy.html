Privacy Policy

Last updated: 13 November 2026

This policy explains what information NXT Foundry collects through www.nxtfoundry.in, why we collect it, who processes it for us, and how to contact us about it. NXT Foundry is based in Bengaluru, India. Questions about this policy go to hello@nxtfoundry.in.

Summary

  • When you send an enquiry, we collect the details you type into the contact form and use them only to respond to you.
  • We do not sell your information or use it for advertising.
  • The website uses Vercel Web Analytics and Speed Insights, which do not set cookies. Google Analytics sets cookies only if you accept them in the cookie banner. Otherwise it records visits without cookies.
  • Files you open in the Vendor Payable Reconciliation tool are processed in your browser and are not uploaded to us.
  • We do not currently use any AI service to process information from this website.

What is added automatically

  • A submission reference and timestamp, so a retried submission is not stored twice and we can refer to your enquiry.
  • A hashed version of your IP address. Your IP address is converted into a keyed hash (HMAC-SHA-256) on our server. The raw IP address is not stored, logged or passed on. The hash is used only to limit how many enquiries can be sent from one connection in a short time.
  • A hashed version of your email address (SHA-256), used only to limit repeated submissions from the same address.
  • How long the form took to fill in, and a hidden field that people never see. Both are basic checks against automated spam.

Why we use it

To read and reply to your enquiry, to follow up on the conversation you started, and to protect the form from spam and abuse. We do not add you to a mailing list.

Where it goes

  • The form sends your details to our server, hosted on Vercel.
  • From there, the details are stored in a Google Sheet in NXT Foundry's Google Workspace account, through Google Apps Script.
  • An email notification with your enquiry is sent to our inbox (hello@nxtfoundry.in) through Gmail.

Our server logs record only whether a submission succeeded or failed. They do not contain your form entries or your IP address.

How long we keep it

We have not set a fixed deletion schedule. We keep enquiries while they are useful for the conversation they started. You can ask us to delete yours at any time (see section 6).

2. The reconciliation tool

The Vendor Payable Reconciliation tool opens with sample data. Ledger files (CSV or Excel), PDFs and other documents you open in the tool are read and processed in your browser. They are not uploaded to NXT Foundry or sent to any other service, and they are not kept after you close or reload the page.

Features marked ✦ in the tool are previews of AI features that are not switched on. They do not send any data. If we switch them on, we will update this policy first.

3. Analytics and cookies

Vercel Web Analytics and Speed Insights

We use Vercel Web Analytics and Vercel Speed Insights to understand which pages are visited and how fast they load. These tools do not use cookies. They collect information such as the page visited, the referring site, browser and device type, operating system, approximate country, and page-load performance. We use this information only in aggregate.

Google Analytics

The website loads Google Tag Manager, which runs Google Analytics 4, on every visit. It uses Google's Consent Mode, so how it works depends on your choice in the cookie banner.

  • If you click “Not now” or ignore the banner, Google Analytics sets no cookies. It still sends Google a basic, cookieless record of your visit, such as the page viewed, the time, your browser and device type, and your approximate location derived from your IP address. Without a cookie, it cannot recognise you on later visits. Google may use these records to estimate overall visitor numbers.
  • If you click “Let’s do it”, Google Analytics also sets first-party cookies (named _ga and _ga_<ID>) that let it recognise repeat visits from the same browser and see how visitors move between pages.

Advertising and ad-personalisation settings are always off. We use the reports to understand how the website is used and to improve it.

How to control cookies

  • On this website. Use the Cookie settings link at the bottom of every page of the main website to change your choice at any time. If you choose “Not now” after accepting, the Google Analytics cookies are deleted straight away.
  • In your browser. Every major browser lets you block or delete cookies, either for all websites or only for www.nxtfoundry.in:
    • Chrome: Settings → Privacy and security → Third-party cookies / Site settings → Cookies and site data
    • Edge: Settings → Cookies and site permissions → Manage and delete cookies and site data
    • Firefox: Settings → Privacy & Security → Cookies and Site Data
    • Safari (Mac): Settings → Privacy → Manage Website Data; Safari (iPhone/iPad): Settings → Safari → Advanced → Website Data
    Clearing “site data” for www.nxtfoundry.in also removes the local storage items listed above, including your banner choice, so the banner will appear again.
  • Block Google Analytics completely. Google offers the Google Analytics Opt-out Browser Add-on, which stops Google Analytics on every website, including the cookieless visit records described above. Many content and tracking blockers do the same.
  • Private browsing. In a private or incognito window, cookies and browser storage are deleted when you close the window.

Blocking cookies or browser storage does not stop the website or the contact form from working.

4. Other services and storage in your browser

  • Hosting. The website is hosted on Vercel. Like any web host, Vercel receives technical information, such as your IP address and browser details, when your browser requests a page.
  • Fonts. The website loads its typeface from Google Fonts, so your browser connects to Google's servers, which receive your IP address and browser details.
  • Browser storage. Apart from the Google Analytics cookies described above, which are set only with your consent, we do not set cookies. The website keeps your cookie-banner choice and a few small settings in your browser's local storage or session storage, such as whether you have seen a tool's guided tour, which summary panels you have collapsed, and the products you picked on the Products page so they can be filled into the contact form. These stay on your device, and you can clear them through your browser settings.

5. Who we share information with

We share information only with the service providers named above, and only so they can provide their services to us: Vercel (hosting and analytics) and Google (Google Workspace, Gmail, Google Fonts and Google Analytics). These providers may process data on servers outside India. We do not sell personal information. We may disclose information if the law requires us to.

6. Your choices and contacting us

You can write to hello@nxtfoundry.in to:

  • ask what information we hold about you from an enquiry,
  • ask us to correct it, or
  • ask us to delete it.

It helps to include your enquiry reference (it starts with “NXT-”) if you have it. You can also contact us by email instead of using the contact form.

7. Changes to this policy

If we change how we handle information, for example by moving enquiries to a different system, we will update this page and the date at the top.
